Tactile Button Lab

Sixty interface elements that behave like real things: fluid, paper, glass, fire, ink, rubber, light and water. Every one is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript in a single file, free to use in your own work.

Best with a mouse. Touch works too. Several play a little sound once you tap them.
